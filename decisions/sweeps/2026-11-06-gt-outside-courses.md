## Top 10 for this student

1. **CS 7648 Interactive Robot Learning** — HRI/LfD with human-subject experiments; best robotics+music bridge (after CS 7641).
2. **ECE 6122 Advanced Programming (C++)** — closes the C++ gap with GPU/sockets, useful for real-time audio and ROS2 nodes.
3. **PSYC 6018 Research Design** (or PSYC 7101) — experimental design for any human-subjects music-cognition or HRI study.
4. **ISYE 6414 Regression** — approachable statistics for user studies and EEG feature analysis.
5. **CS 6750 HCI** — no prereqs; foundational user research methods, cross-listed with PSYC.
6. **ECE 6254 Statistical ML** — signal-processing flavored ML for EEG decoding; verify the section runs.
7. **ECE 6255 Speech Signal Processing** — the deepest DSP-for-audio course; requires DSP (ECE 4270) background.
8. **CS 7470 Mobile & Ubiquitous Computing** — wearable sensing projects; natural fit for instrument/biosignal interfaces.
9. **CS 6456 UI Software** — building interactive systems and toolkits for music-tech prototypes.
10. **CS 7642 Reinforcement Learning** — for expressive/improvisational robot behavior; take after ML.

**Permits:** no found syllabus states non-major restrictions; ID studios and GT-Lorraine CS 7630 are effectively program-limited; ECE/CS sections fill with majors first, so request permits early.
